2. Sample Assignment

Topic: Artificial Intelligence in Everyday Life

Aim: To understand Artificial Intelligence and its applications in everyday life.

Artificial Intelligence is a technology that enables computers to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

Include in Your Assignment:

  1. Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
  2. How AI Works
  3. Applications of AI
  4. Advantages of AI
  5. Limitations of AI
  6. Conclusion

3. Assignment Questions

  1. What is Artificial Intelligence?
  2. Write any three applications of AI.
  3. How is AI used in mobile phones?
  4. What are the advantages of AI?
  5. Mention one limitation of AI.
  6. How is AI useful in education?
  7. What did you learn from this assignment?
